# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- Obsidian’s built-in sidebar vault switcher now follows the Vault Dock
  switching mode and can reopen windows hidden by the plugin.
- Change the mode directly from the built-in menu. The menu marks the active
  mode and uses the same shared or per-vault setting as Vault Dock.
- The switching mode setting now explains that it also affects the built-in
  switcher.
- The color picker preview matches the selected tile’s size and label
  visibility.
- Tiles with labels are 15% narrower, with bolder text and more space below
  the icon. Labels wrap to two lines, and the horizontal dock grows as needed
  while keeping icons aligned along the top.
- Show or hide tile labels from the dock’s position menu. The toggle uses the
  same setting as the plugin settings tab.
- Added four animated demos to both READMEs covering vault switching, dock
  placement, tile resizing, and tile images.

## 0.1.0

- Open or focus any registered vault with one click from a dock. Place the dock
  at the top, bottom, left, or right. It stays visible on the left or right when
  the sidebars are collapsed.
- Resize tiles by dragging the dock edge or using the settings slider. Hide
  labels for compact square tiles.
- Add, replace, or remove tile images from the context menu. Tiles without
  images show vault initials with customizable background and text colors.
- Choose whether to hide the current window and keep its tabs, close it after
  the destination is ready, or keep both windows open. In hide and close modes,
  the destination takes the current window’s size and position.
- Share the switching mode and tile images across vaults or set them separately
  for each vault. Appearance and language settings apply across the profile.
- Follow Obsidian’s language or choose English, Russian, Kazakh, Belarusian,
  Uzbek, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, or Japanese.

Requires Obsidian Desktop 1.15.0 or later. If you installed an earlier build
under `vault-switcher-bar`, follow the [installation notes](README.md#installation)
to switch to `vault-dock` and keep your settings and images.
